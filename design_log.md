# Design log

One or two lines per part: what you tried, what failed, what you changed. Include at least one failure.

- Part 0: Recorded the words, sentences and room-noise blocks using the personal read-aloud code. Ran the splitting and audit workflow and retained the original recordings, clips and audit outputs.
- Part 1: Implemented MFCC extraction from first principles using NumPy and SciPy. Initially, Mel filterbank scaling differed from Librosa; corrected the frequency conversion and filterbank implementation, achieving close numerical agreement. Implemented autocorrelation F0 estimation and compared it with pYIN; refined pitch selection using normalization and continuity checks, then saved comparison plots and result files.
- Part 2: Implemented diagonal-Gaussian log emissions, Forward/Viterbi algorithms in log space, and segmental K-means for ten three-state HMMs. Trained for five iterations while reserving repetition 1 for testing; achieved 9/10 (90%) held-out accuracy. The word “think” was misclassified as “sink”; saved the confusion matrix.
- Part 3: Implemented the CTC forward algorithm from first principles using NumPy and log-space dynamic programming, including blank expansion and the repeated-label transition restriction. Verified against PyTorch CTCLoss: NumPy 0.3073 versus PyTorch approximately 0.30729994 (absolute difference approximately 5.82e-08).
- Part 4: Implemented add-one and interpolated Kneser-Ney models, evaluated Brown corpus perplexity, integrated CMUdict and G2P fallback, and recorded three weighted personal pronunciation alternatives.
- Part 5: Built the toy lexicon with 10 states; hand determinisation gives 7 states and minimisation gives 5. The notebook contains three toy phone-to-word paths and L∘G composition for two sentences. A clean-kernel rerun is still required before treating composition and own-sentence decoding as fully verified.
- Part 6: Precomputed wav2vec2 posteriors and Whisper transcripts; implemented WER and CTC prefix beam search with language-model fusion. Selected alpha = 0.0 because all tested weights produced the same tuning WER. Evaluated held-out sentences at beam widths 1 and 8 and both recognisers under clean, 10 dB and 0 dB conditions. Whisper average WER: 0.3978/0.4291/0.5084; wav2vec2: 0.4276/0.7798/0.9489. Beam-width 8 averaged 444.06 ms versus 45.98 ms for width 1.
- Part 7: Implemented written-to-spoken normalisation and obtained 4/4 exact matches on the four configured sentences. Inverted the Mel filterbank using its pseudo-inverse and evaluated Griffin–Lim at 1, 10 and 30 iterations; 30-iteration convergence is 0.1581709. Generated four Tacotron2-WaveRNN outputs and assessed them by listening. Griffin–Lim 30-iteration STOI against the original recording is 0.9204; no direct Neural TTS STOI is claimed because the independently synthesised output is not time-aligned with the original.
